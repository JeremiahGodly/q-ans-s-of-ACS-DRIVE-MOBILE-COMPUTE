1. SCR Conduction Intervals

Thyristors $T_1, T_2$ fire at trigger angle $\alpha$ and conduct until $\pi + \alpha$. Thyristors $T_3, T_4$ fire at $\pi + \alpha$ and conduct until $2\pi + \alpha$. Continuous armature current keeps SCRs ON even when $v_s$ drops below zero!

2. Governing Equation (Framed 30%)

Average DC terminal voltage across armature: $$V_{dc} = \frac{1}{\pi} \int_{\alpha}^{\pi+\alpha} V_m \sin(\omega t)\, d(\omega t) = \frac{2 V_m}{\pi} \cos \alpha$$ Motor Speed equation: $\omega_m = \frac{V_{dc} - I_a R_a}{K_t \Phi}$

3. The 3 Pitfalls Examiners Penalize

• Reversed $E_b$: Always place $+$ at the top brush to oppose incoming current.
• Missing $D_{fw}$: The freewheeling diode prevents negative voltage spikes across thyristors.
• Missing Gate Pulses: Clearly mark gate leads $g_1, g_2, g_3, g_4$.
